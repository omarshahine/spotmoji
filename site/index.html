How it works

Three keystrokes from idea to emoji.

Use direct Spotlight results for the fastest path, or open the compact picker when you want to browse.

Open Spotlight

Press Command-Space from any app with a text field.

Describe it

Type something like Spotmoji threads, rocket, or happy tears.

Press Return

Spotmoji copies the result, restores your app, pastes, and quietly quits.

Good to know

Small app, clear boundaries.

Does the picker actually live inside Spotlight?

Individual emoji appear as native Spotlight results and paste directly. macOS does not let third-party apps embed a live custom picker inside the Spotlight window, so the generic Search Spotmoji action opens the compact native picker with your query carried over.

Why does Spotmoji need Accessibility permission?

Only to restore the app you were using and send Command-V. If you do not grant it, selecting an emoji still copies it to the pasteboard.

Does it send my searches anywhere?

No. Search, ranking, the Core Spotlight index, and paste handling all happen locally. Spotmoji has no analytics, account, or network API.

Will it be available in the Mac App Store?

That is planned. Homebrew is the first distribution channel while the direct signing, notarization, and release flow settles.
